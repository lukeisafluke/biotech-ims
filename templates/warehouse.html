How Catalog Sync Works:
  • Scans all sheets in the workbook for product numbers (Cat. No. / SKU), product names, sizes, and CAD prices.
  • Assumes this uploaded sheet belongs to the selected supplier above.
  • Splits hyphenated product numbers (e.g. A101-01) into SKU (A101) and Sub-SKU (01).
  • Smart Diffing: Only adds new SKUs or updates existing SKUs whose name, size, or price changed — never duplicates products.
